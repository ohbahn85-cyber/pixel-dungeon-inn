The Roguelike Characters pack is included for completeness; heroes and monsters
on screen currently use Tiny Dungeon sprites. Slot symbols for items (weapons,
potions, food, ore, gems) also use tiles from these packs. Abstract symbols
such as 7, lock, runes, and reward multipliers remain original.
